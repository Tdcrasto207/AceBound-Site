Our system

The 5-Step Process That Makes Cold Outbound Actually Work for Sell-Side M&A Firms

This is a high level overview. The full detail on each step is covered in the video above.

01

Manual

Define the Buy Box Accurately

We define with precision: Niche, Geography, Revenue Band, and any exclusions, then layer in intent signals. This type of precision is what finds viable prospects instead of keeping it general and just praying.

02

Manual

Set up Email Outreach Infrastructure

3 to 5 sending domains separate from your primary, 2 to 3 mailboxes on each, SPF, DKIM and DMARC configured on every one, and 14 to 21 days of warmup. Your firm's main domain never carries campaign risk.

03

Manual / AI Driven

Build Target List

Every company on the list gets its actual website and LinkedIn read. Services, markets, location, years in business, hiring signals, leadership, credentials and recent announcements, extracted as fields the databases are not doing.

04

Manual / AI Driven

Deploy Personalized Campaign

Every email is written off what we pulled and then enriched, not just the industry code and revenue. Multiple sequences run against split segments of the list. Whichever wins becomes the principal sequence.

05

Recommended to Firm

Utilize CTA Item

Asking for a 30 minute call is a heavy first ask. Instead we recommend that you lead with an Exit Readiness Scorecard that grades their business across a few value drivers specific to your niche. This is now an offer instead of an ask.
